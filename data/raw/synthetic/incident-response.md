# Service Incident Response Procedure

## Severity classification

A SEV-1 incident is a complete production outage or a confirmed
exposure of customer data.

A SEV-2 incident is a partial service disruption with an available
workaround and no confirmed customer data exposure.

## Initial response

For SEV-1 incidents, notify the on-call engineer and incident commander
within 10 minutes of detection.

The incident commander coordinates the response and assigns
investigation tasks.

## Communication

During a SEV-1 incident, provide a status update every 30 minutes.
Each update must describe the impact, current actions, and the time
of the next update.

## Review

Complete a post-incident review within three business days after
the incident is resolved.
