# Internal System Access Policy

## Access requests

Employees must request access through the internal service desk.
Each request must include the target system, business justification,
and required access duration.

## Approval

The employee's manager and the system owner must approve the request.
Production administrator access also requires security team approval.

## Temporary access

Temporary access expires after seven calendar days unless a shorter
duration is specified in the approved request.

Extensions require a new approval before the existing access expires.

## Employee departure

When an employee leaves the company, IT must revoke their access
within four hours of receiving the departure notification from HR.
